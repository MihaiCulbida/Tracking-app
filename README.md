# Tracking-app

A web app for tracking routes on an interactive map. It records your location in real time, draws your route on the map and shows your distance, time and speed.

## How to use

1. Open the app and press **Start**. Allow location access when the browser asks.
2. Move around. Your route is drawn on the map and the stats update live.
3. Press **Finish** when you are done.

## Stats

- **Distance**: how far you have traveled (km).
- **Time**: how long the tracking has been running (hh:mm:ss).
- **Speed**: your current speed (km/h).

## Buttons

- **Start**: begins tracking your location. Press it again to pause and resume.
- **Finish**: ends the tracking session.
- **Follow me**: keeps the map centered on your position. Press it again to turn it off and move the map freely.
- **Save image**: saves the map with your route as an image.
